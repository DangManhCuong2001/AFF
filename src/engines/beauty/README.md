# Beauty & Skincare Video Engine

## Overview
Engine dedicated to Cosmetics, Skincare, Haircare, and Personal Care for TikTok.

## Core Selling Mechanism
- **ROUTINE & TEXTURE EXPERIENCE**: Highlights immediate application feel, skin absorption, ingredient benefits, and realistic finish.

## Planned Formats
- `texture-application`: Smooth macro spread of creams, serums, and tints.
- `skincare-routine`: Step-by-step regimen placement (Cleanse → Treat → Hydrate).
- `wear-test`: Long-lasting smudge proofing and sweat resistance.

## Compliance
- Enforces strict compliance against unverifiable medical or instant cure claims.

## Status
- Planned for future release.
