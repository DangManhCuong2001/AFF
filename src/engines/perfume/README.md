# Perfume & Fragrance Video Engine

## Overview
Engine dedicated to Fragrances, Body Mists, and Scented Room Diffusers.

## Core Selling Mechanism
- **LIFESTYLE, VIBE & SCENT NOTES**: Conveys olfactory experience through cinematic moods, top/heart/base notes breakdowns, longevity expectations, and compliment triggers.

## Planned Formats
- `vibe-check`: Scene matching (e.g., date night, office fresh, summer vacation).
- `notes-breakdown`: Visual breakdown of notes (citrus, woody, amber, floral).
- `blind-sniff-reaction`: Simulated or real crowd reactions and projection tests.
